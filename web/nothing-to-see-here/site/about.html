Our Story

Meridian started in a two-car garage with a single delivery van and a dream: no office should ever run out of staples again. Nearly three decades later we operate two regional warehouses and a small but mighty IT team that keeps this very website running.

Our infrastructure is intentionally simple. We believe in boring, dependable technology — the same way we believe in boring, dependable staplers. If it isn't broke, we don't fix it.

Our Mission

Keep offices running smoothly, one delivery at a time.
